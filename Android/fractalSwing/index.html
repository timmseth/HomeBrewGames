fractalSwing

An interactive fractal animation generator. Eleven fractal families, every parameter on a swing you tune while it runs, and audio sync so the whole thing moves to your music. The background of this page is the real thing.

Playable in your browser. The Android build hears what your phone is playing.
▶ Play in your browser ⬇ Download for Android · v1.0.0
0.9 MB · Android 8.0+ · WebGL2 on the web · no ads, no tracking · how to use & install · changelog

Eleven fractals

Quadratic, Burning Ship, Tricorn, Multibrot, Phoenix, Celtic, Ducks, Kali, Newton, Box Fold and Sine, each in Julia or Mandelbrot form.

Sixteen swings

Centre, amplitude, period, shape and phase for the constant, zoom, rotation, colour, orbit trap, kaleidoscope and feedback. Nudge them live.

Audio sync

Beats kick the zoom and flash the screen, bass shifts the colour, treble feeds the glow. Any swing can ride any band.

Trippy FX

Video feedback trails, kaleidoscope, glow, chromatic aberration, grain and scanlines, all on their own sliders.

Twelve looks

From Julia Pendulum to Feedback Storm. Randomize, mutate, save your own, and copy a look between the phone and the browser.

Pure GPU

Fragment-shader fractals in OpenGL ES 3 on Android and WebGL2 on the web, with adaptive resolution to stay smooth.
