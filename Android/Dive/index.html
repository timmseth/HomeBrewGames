Dive

A zen, tilt-controlled descent into an endless ocean that slowly becomes a sea of fractals. Eat what's smaller, avoid what's bigger, reshape yourself, and sink as deep as you dare.

Flow, tuned to you

Depth is difficulty. A hidden director keeps the challenge right at your edge, never boring, never brutal.

Always a bigger fish

Everything is sized relative to you. Grow 8× and the ocean quietly rescales around you.

Spore-style Shaper

Drag jaws, fins, eyes, spikes, glow and Brood Pods onto your body. Every part changes how you swim.

The Brood

Harvesters tether prey and reel it in, even while the app is closed. Seven idle upgrade tracks.

Music visualizer

Autopilot drifts on its own while the fractal sea pulses to Dive's generated lofi, your phone's music, or a folder of tracks.

Zen stakes

You never die. Bites cost segments, which regrow. One hand, portrait, tilt or a floating joystick.
