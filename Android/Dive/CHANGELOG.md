# Dive — changelog

Only the latest APK is kept in this repo. Every version installs over the previous one and keeps your save.

## 1.3.0 — The Brood, autopilot, music visualizer · 2026-11-03
- **Brood Pods and harvesters.** Harvesters tether prey and reel it into your mouth.
- **Brood tab** with 7 upgrades: Reach, Speed, Grip, Haul, Instinct, Regrowth, Drift.
- **Offline gathering**, with a "While you drifted" summary when you come back.
- **Autopilot** (AUTO pill): a cinematic drifting camera, a fading HUD and optional auto-evolve. The difficulty system doesn't learn from autopilot play.
- **Music visualizer:** Dive lofi, phone audio (e.g. Spotify) or a music folder. The fractal sea, glow, harvesters and plankton react to the beat.
- **Music engine:** an ambient drift arrangement, a generative melody, voice-leading and slow key changes.

## 1.2.1 — Farther cameras and Settings · 2026-11-02
- The top-down camera sits 1.5× higher, and the dive view is 2× farther back.
- New Settings screen with live values: cameras, field of view, tilt, joystick, hold time, smoothing, turn speed, haptics, music and graphics quality.

## 1.2.0 — Joystick and smoother controls · 2026-11-02
- Floating virtual joystick: drag anywhere to steer. Only a finger held still switches the view.
- Smoother tilt, turning, banking and camera.
- Pause menu and journal, discoveries, edge arrows, heartbeat haptic, leviathan wakes, bubbles, part info, 🎲 Mutate and orb labels.

## 1.1.0 — One-hand play and drag-and-drop Shaper · 2026-11-02
- Hold to switch to dive view.
- Tap to aim lunges.
- A hungrier, magnetic mouth.
- Spore-style drag-and-drop Shaper.
- Ready-to-eat glow, eat sparks, combo melody, living body animation, adaptive graphics and a gentle first minute.

## 1.0.1 — Playable alpha · 2026-11-02
- First public build.
- Zen eating: swim into prey to eat it, with tapping as an optional lunge.
- Fixes from code review and an economy rebalance.

## 1.0.0 — First compile · 2026-11-02
- Tilt swimming, strikes, flight dive, two-layer DDA, 8 creature types, Shaper, fractal sea and generated lofi.
